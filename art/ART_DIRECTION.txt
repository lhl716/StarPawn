StarPawn Art and UI Handoff

Goal
Make every object feel at home in the original game's worn science-fiction pawnshop. The current high-resolution, photorealistic product images clash with the pixel-art environment. Use a gold-ring sample to establish pixel scale, silhouette, metallic highlights, and defect readability before expanding the set. The user must approve the style before full production.

First-round deliverable only
Create one original gold ring in three states: intact, worn plating exposing the base metal, and a cracked band. Keep composition, silhouette, viewing angle, and lighting identical. Include a 64 x 64 inventory preview and a 384 x 256 inspection preview. Present the three states side by side for comparison and retain a separate transparent PNG for each. Submit proposals under art/proposals/; do not replace runtime images or change gameplay code in this round.

Style direction
Take inspiration from the base game's muted, aged industrial setting: warm paper, ink blue, gray-green, and oxidized copper. Use gold to emphasize value and vermilion for exceptional or error feedback. Build clear pixel clusters with a limited tonal range and restrained dithering. Avoid photographic surfaces, blown-out glows, plastic-looking metal, and excessive empty canvas. An aged setting does not mean every object is damaged: intact items must read clearly as intact.
Do not reproduce other artists' images, real jewelry brands, or payment-network trademarks. Use a fictional bank for debit and credit cards, with no real account details.

Image and export contract
1. The current inspection image has an approximately 3:2 aspect ratio and a logical frame of 298 x 199. A 384 x 256 pixel master is recommended. If a 1536 x 1024 export is needed, upscale by 4 using nearest-neighbor sampling. Confirm the final scale during the first review.
2. Paper illustrations require transparency and enough margin to preserve the entire object. Items images need a separately readable small-size treatment, rather than an unreviewed downscale of the large image. Standard 1 x 1 inventory cells use 64 x 64 images; 2 x 1 cards use 128 x 64. Confirm other footprints against the final item metadata.
3. Normal, defective, instrument-observation, and repaired states of the same item must align pixel for pixel. Preserve the silhouette, camera, lighting, and canvas.
4. Defects are defined, fixed templates combined through allowed slots. Do not generate arbitrary random noise or freely drifting cracks. Handbook examples, runtime images, hotspots, and cause IDs must agree.
5. Deliver a base object layer, an object mask, transparent defect overlays, optional instrument views, optional repaired images, and hotspots.json. Defect layers must stay inside the object mask. Interior layers of rough jade appear only in the corresponding observation state.
6. Hotspot U/V and Width/Height are normalized to 0..1 with a top-left origin. The actual configuration fields are ViewId (front/window) and InstrumentKind (scale/assay/loupe/jade_light/uv or empty). Do not substitute an invented visibleMode field. CauseId groups evidence for the same underlying issue; Id distinguishes individual observations. TemplateId links to a fixed defect explanation.
7. Text is rendered by the game. Do not bake amounts, daily limits, purity values, or long Chinese text into UI backgrounds. Handbook text is variable: provide page templates and illustrations. English documentation does not change the game's Chinese localization.
8. Use lowercase snake_case filenames and stable prefixes. Place editable source files and layers in art/source; runtime PNGs belong in mod/Textures/Items and mod/Textures/Paper. Record relationships in asset-plan.csv and the updated mapping. Preserve other contributors' gameplay changes.

Full art scope after approval
Jewelry: gold and silver rings, bracelets, necklaces, and earrings; gold and silver bars; everyday, crafted, and set-stone designs. Fixed templates may cover intact condition, wear, fractures, plating, and inconsistent hallmarks, with valid combinations.
Jade: at least six distinct rough-stone skins, origins, or quality groups; whole stones, opened windows, transmitted-light views, and cut surfaces; pendants, cabochons, bangles, and bead strands. Six different names must not reuse the same stone image.
Instruments: electronic scale, purity analyzer, jeweler's loupe, jade inspection light, and UV observation lamp; idle, active, complete, and invalid states. Visuals should communicate observation conditions without revealing every unknown internal result.
Banking: debit card, credit card, card holder, and physical terminal; front/back and valid/void states. Limits are displayed in the UI; each card only references an account.
Handbooks: master index plus volumes for precious metals, jade, antiques, paintings, electronics, media, and books; covers and open-page templates. Include the physical appraisal-manual organizer with empty, partially filled, and open states, with readable tabs.
Other items: restyle vases, paintings, consoles, handhelds, cartridges, discs, books, and certificates as a coherent set.

UI scope
Business hub; visitor catalog; owned inventory; inspection desk; instrument readings; evidence/cause summary; quote confirmation; asset records/certificates; repair and jade processing; banking/physical cards; credit/loans; securities/market/holdings; news/nightly settlement; pawn ledger; handbook index/volumes; manual storage; feedback, error, and locked states.
After the item sample is approved, first create static layouts for the inspection desk and banking page. Then deliver UI tokens, nine-slice backgrounds, button states, icons, typography hierarchy, and spacing specifications. Validate at a minimum baseline of 1080p and provide 4K previews with integer scaling where applicable.

Interactions to preserve
Two consecutive misses end the current inspection opportunity. Customer dialogue and actions communicate this state. Discovered evidence supports multiple selections, with duplicate causes merged. At maximum appraisal level, currently visible evidence is recorded automatically. Clearly distinguish owned, customer-owned, and consigned items.
Financial pages show balances, debt, today's usage and limits, interest, and the next settlement date. Numbers and action feedback need stable positions; distribute workflows across appropriate pages.

Review and integration stages
Stage 1: item style sample, approved by the user.
Stage 2: representative rough jade, cards, manual organizer, and two UI layouts to confirm a consistent visual system.
Stage 3: complete each category in asset-plan.csv. The gameplay branch handles state binding, hotspots, save compatibility, and in-game verification.

Current gameplay and file mapping
Version 0.3 has integrated 239 catalog/state definitions, five instruments, seven category handbooks, and an eight-slot organizer. See docs/REVISION_03.txt for the actual delivery scope.
art/runtime-art-map.json is authoritative for current IDs, textures, inventory footprints, fixed evidence, and masks. It also includes two physical cards. existing-assets.json and existing-items.json are historical 0.2 snapshots only; localized names in generated snapshots identify actual game content.
asset-plan.csv describes design scope and includes visual proposals that are not implemented, such as a card holder, physical terminal, and instrument animation states. Its proposed filenames are not current runtime IDs. See art/UI_SPEC.txt for screen and interaction-state requirements.
New development images use the sp3_ prefix under mod/Textures. They may be replaced, but states within each family must remain aligned. development/masks and geometry-contract.json describe development geometry constraints, not an approved art style.
Start from the current 0.3 code on main, then create art/style-sample. Do not begin the complete art set from the historical 0.2 commit.
source/Content/Generate-Catalog.py generates data/localization by default; --development-art overwrites development images. source/Art/Generate-Art.py is the old generator. After art integration, avoid generators that overwrite approved assets. Export-ArtMap.py can update the mapping independently.
