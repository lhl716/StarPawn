Native features to match
The main illustrated reference is a tall warm gray-cream paper sheet on the left of the shop, with a dark pixel outline, irregular lightly worn edge, restrained folded/worn corners, and no dashboard frame. It leaves the counter/item context visible alongside it.
A small blue issuer line and thin blue rules precede a larger centered dark title. Section headings are bold muted blue. Compact dark body copy explains a check; visual examples sit immediately below it. Some sections are full width; paired comparisons occupy two columns. Thin blue table dividers organize letter/mark comparisons. Green reference labels and red problem labels are accompanied by words, never color alone. A faint issuer stamp appears behind otherwise legible content. Close and duplicate/window controls sit at the top right.
The separate mentor note uses ruled paper and a red margin; it is a secondary reference for job notes, not the template for the illustrated appraisal pages. Do not put ruled notebook lines behind every gem photograph. Do not replace reference documents with a terminal, statistical cards, large gold chrome or a modern tabbed analytics dashboard.

Required content per template
Category/form, reference appearance, the exact authored problem, where to compare, necessary tool/view, what it suggests, what it cannot prove, price component affected, and whether repair is supported. A manual teaches fixed category templates; it never reads the current item's private truth or lists its undiscovered active defects. Each example links TemplateId, model, view, layer path and crop bounds.
Instrument-only findings have a separate sample reading/interpretation diagram, not an invented visible flaw on the base object. Screeners can refer a stone for further testing; natural versus laboratory-grown origin is not established by sparkle or a generic UV glow. Separate truth, claim and suspicion. Appraisal findings and manufacturing state are different: an empty service setting is not a counterfeit complete ring.
All 250 templates need traceable examples across the appropriate category books. docs/HANDBOOK_INDEX_04.csv fixes book ownership, chapter order, example model, all five TemplateIds and the 2/2/1 pagination for each of the 50 chapters. Page 0 is the book index. Nine category manuals: metals, jade, antiques, art, electronics, media, books, recovery/refining, jewelry/gemstones. Metal and mounting pages may cross-reference one another but cannot merge their different template libraries. Legacy manual compatibility remains explicit.
