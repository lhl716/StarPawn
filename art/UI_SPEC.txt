StarPawn 0.3 UI Art Specification (style awaiting user approval)

Work sequence
Complete the ring sample in CLAUDE_STYLE_PROMPT.txt first. After approval, present static mockups for the inspection desk and banking page, then expand to the remaining screens below. Prioritize skins and visual treatment. Submit interaction-layout changes as separate code changes and verify them in the game.

Shared layout
PaperUI currently uses a 600 x 400 design canvas, with ordinary content between y=74 and y=330. The title and subtitle sit at the top, with workflow feedback and navigation at the bottom. The inspection image occupies x=49, y=99, w=298, h=199. Proposed layouts may change this arrangement, but must not crop the item image or move hotspots without updating their coordinates.
Typography hierarchy: title 18, body 11–13, secondary text 10. Validate Chinese text at 1080p and 4K scaling; shrinking a screenshot is not a substitute for testing legibility. Include examples with long names, debt, amounts of four or more digits, no data, and failed actions.
Proposed starting palette: warm paper #E7DDC5, ink #25222B, ink blue #28465A, gray-green #6B8880, gold #D7AB54, and error vermilion #A64741. Success and failure require text or icons as well as color.
Deliver text-free backgrounds; default/hover/pressed/disabled buttons; selected pagination/filter states; tabs; evidence outlines; handbook-page templates; and empty/locked states. Amounts, purity, quotas, and body text are rendered by the game. English documentation does not change Chinese in-game text.

Screens
1. Business hub: appraisal level, XP, today's gold/silver sell and buyback prices; entry points for trading, inventory, banking, securities, pawning, financial overview, and the supply shop.
2. Trade catalog and inventory: four category tabs for all items, precious metals, jade, and collectibles/supplies; five rows per page; category, owned/consigned/consumed state, and pagination. Action labels vary by instrument, handbook, or merchandise type.
3. Inspection desk: complete item image, fixed evidence hotspots, discovered markers, multiple bargaining reasons, material weight, current observation view, handbook, instruments, processing, and quoting. Two consecutive misses trigger feedback that the customer has withdrawn the item; owned items use eye-strain feedback. Preserve the complete object silhouette.
4. Instrument desk: five instrument rows, clearly distinguishing not owned, not applicable, not measured, and recorded. Jade-light readings retain uncertainty; do not imply hidden grades or yields through UI cues.
5. Quote and confirmation: buying/selling direction, material price floor, craftsmanship, deduplicated evidence causes, final price, and market date. Expired-quote and insufficient-funds errors must be readable.
6. Asset record and certificate: identity, origin, known evidence, and measured/unmeasured material properties. Certificates must not reveal unidentified results. Book text and reading pagination remain separate.
7. Workshop: repairable damage, window opening, four jade product types, fees, and processed state. Make expected output and failure conditions clear.
8. Banking and cards: physical-card thumbnail, valid serial, and account balance; daily deposit/withdrawal usage and remaining quotas; account daily rate and next-day eligibility for new deposits. Reissuing a card voids the old card. Do not use real account details.
9. Credit and loans: principal, interest, contractual rate, due date, today's remaining borrowing quota, and total credit limit. Repayment must remain accessible. Distinguish existing contractual rates from rates offered on new contracts.
10. Securities and news: daily buy/sell prices, holdings, liquidation value, market changes, and related news. Limit orders and derivatives are not implemented; do not depict them as available actions.
11. Financial overview and daily settlement: cash, deposits, securities liquidation value, debt, net funds, separately listed pawn receivables, and a settlement summary. Make clear that inventory is excluded from net funds.
12. Pawn contracts: lending/borrowing direction, collateral, status, rate, maturity, redemption/disposal, empty lists, and settled states.
13. Handbooks: seven covers, introductory pages, and fixed-defect example pages. Mark observation areas and explain tools, repairability, and economic causes. Include page numbers and a return path to the current inspection desk.
14. Organizer: native eight-slot book container with insert/remove/read actions and stored-state markers. Empty it before moving or selling it. Keep the native container's appearance consistent with the base game; do not depict a separate, nonfunctional inventory.
15. Supply shop: five rows of purchasable physical items, prices, functions, owned counts, and pagination. Insufficient funds and full-inventory failures preserve existing items.

Delivery locations

art/source/: editable source files and layers after approval.
mod/Textures/Paper/: approved, integrated runtime skins only; do not overwrite them ahead of approval.
