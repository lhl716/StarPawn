StarPawn 0.3.0 Gameplay Delivery and Art Handoff
2026-11-13; Windows; game version 100-Rev13; Steam BuildID 26525552.

Implemented in this revision
1. Five categories in both gold and silver: rings, bracelets, necklaces, earrings, and bars. Each has Orbit and Starburst designs, for 20 models. Each model has 8 fixed condition combinations (intact, wear, cracks, plating, and combinations), totaling 160 definitions. The original 30 definitions remain available.
2. Six jade rough families: Yellow Sand Skin, Black Sand Skin, Water-Worn, White Salt Skin, Rust Skin, and Mountain Rough. Each has 4 exterior/window defect combinations, totaling 24 definitions. Families have different quality weights and base prices; quality and yield are saved when the physical item is created. Four new finished categories (pendants, cabochons, bangles, and bead strands) each have 3 conditions, totaling 12 definitions. Opening a window or cutting never rerolls the outcome. Bangles require sufficient yield. Legacy rough retains its original outputs.
3. Five purchasable physical instruments: digital scale (120 Cr), purity analyzer (650 Cr), 10x loupe (90 Cr), jade inspection light (180 Cr), and UV lamp (260 Cr). They work from the inspection desk and the vanilla right-click Use action; the player must own the actual instrument. Recorded readings persist. Repeated measurements neither award duplicate XP nor restore a locked inspection opportunity. Light inspection retains uncertainty.
4. Seven physical manuals: Precious Metals, Jade, Antiques, Art, Electronics, Media, and Books, each priced at 65 Cr. They contain fixed template descriptions, example illustrations, location markers, tools, and repairability information. They do not reveal the current item's hidden condition for the player.
5. A physical eight-slot manual organizer, priced at 160 Cr. Actual books can be inserted, removed, and read through the native container window. Child item IDs survive save/reload. An organizer containing books cannot be moved or sold; it must be emptied first to prevent unpriced contents from being traded with it.
6. Debit and credit cards retain the physical account-credential system introduced in 0.2. Reissuing a card invalidates the old serial. New daily limits are 5,000 Cr deposited, 2,000 Cr withdrawn, 1,000 Cr borrowed on credit, and 3,000 Cr borrowed through ordinary loans. Total credit is capped at 5,000 Cr; total outstanding ordinary-loan principal is capped at 10,000 Cr. Repayments do not consume borrowing limits. Reissuing cards, reloading, repaying, and reopening accounts do not reset the day's usage.
7. Daily rates for new agreements: savings 0.20%, credit 0.60%, ordinary loans 0.45%, and pawn contracts 0.60%. Existing contracts retain their agreed rates. New deposits become interest-eligible the following day, fractional remainders are retained, and the last settled day prevents duplicate interest posting.
8. The lobby now includes a financial overview, remaining daily limits, and an overnight settlement receipt. Net funds include cash, deposits, and the sell value of securities, less debt. Pawn lending receivables are listed separately; inventory is excluded. Metal weight, purity, and material valuation still use the shared daily market. The buyback floor remains enforced.
9. The catalog uses category filters and pagination. Each item family offers one fixed condition per day, chosen deterministically from the save and date. Reloading, instruments, and mastery do not reroll it. Upgrading an old save during a day adds new families without restocking previously purchased legacy offers.

Counting definitions
239 catalog definitions = 30 legacy definitions + 160 metal conditions + 24 rough conditions + 12 finished-jade conditions + 5 instruments + 7 manuals + 1 organizer. The two bank cards are registered separately and are not part of the 239. A condition combination is not a separate visual model.

Validation
156 automated business-rule and boundary tests: economy 32, content 29, content bootstrap 11, commerce transactions 27, native trade boundaries 22, bank cards 10, and appraisal/instruments/manuals 25.
An additional 33 Harmony signature/binding metadata checks passed. These checks are not equivalent to executing the patches inside Unity.
Geometry checks passed for 196 new item-condition silhouettes and 284 fixed evidence centers. Metal damage changes remain inside the object masks. This does not constitute approval of the artistic quality.
Compilation against the local game assemblies passed. The existing CS1701 warning remains for unifying the game's Newtonsoft netstandard2.0 reference with the mod's netstandard2.1 target.
In-game slot5 coverage: upgrading a 0.2 save; buying two manuals and an organizer; inserting, removing, reading, and reloading them; buying and testing a 6 g gold-plated ring; browsing six rough families; light inspection; opening a window; cutting and delivering a bead strand; deposit limits; the financial overview; normal sleep from day 3 to day 4; one-time overnight settlement; balances and items surviving reload; and using the native right-click instrument action on a 50 g gold bar.
Hashes of the original slot0-3 saves are unchanged. The independent demo save and full screenshots remain local; personal saves are not uploaded.

Financial reference
Rinka's public description of Deep Space Financial Exchange (original title: "深空金融交易所 Exchange：股票·大宗") was reviewed. See MOD_REFERENCE_RESEARCH.txt for the source. The account overview, organization of holdings/quotes, and overnight settlement feedback informed StarPawn's independent ledger implementation.
No third-party code or assets were copied. The reference mod was not installed, and coexistence compatibility has not been claimed. Limit orders, derivatives, securities-backed loans, and a separate physical financial terminal were not implemented in this revision.

Delivery boundaries
Gameplay code and development assets are integrated. New item images are geometry-validation drafts, and the previous art style still needs replacement. This delivery does not claim final art completion.
Claude should create art/style-sample from the current main branch and make only a three-condition gold-ring sample first. The gameplay/0.3 branch also contains the 0.3 baseline. The full item set and UI follow user approval of that style. The complete design handoff is in art/ART_DIRECTION.txt, UI_SPEC.txt, runtime-art-map.json, and asset-plan.csv.
Testing applies to the baseline above. Long-term economic feel still needs player feedback. Not every combination of third-party mods, display resolution, or native exceptional path has been individually tested in game.
