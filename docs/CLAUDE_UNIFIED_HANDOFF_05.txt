StarPawn: Unified Appraisal, Pawn, Trade, Art, and Copy Handoff
Revision 05 | 2026-11-13 | English implementation contract

READ THIS FIRST

Deliver one coherent update to StarPawn for Probably Stolen. Appraisal is the
entry point to a connected shop business: inspect and value an actual item,
complete an appraisal commission where applicable, then potentially buy it,
accept it as pawn collateral, or return it to the customer. Carry the same item,
observations, market information, and ownership through the entire transaction.
The update includes gameplay code, native integration, artwork, UI, copy,
migrations, balance, and verification. It is not an art-only assignment.

This is one overall requirement, not a sequence of separately approved rounds.
Make routine implementation decisions, inspect the existing code, and complete
the connected behavior. Do not stop at a style sample, a plan, or unintegrated
assets. Report actual limitations rather than claiming unavailable tests passed.

2. Verified starting state and known defects

The playable 0.3.1 build integrated Claude's batch04 artwork into 254 item
definitions, including condition variants and 15 added short manuals. Its prior
171 business/boundary checks and screenshots are historical evidence only.
The full 0.4 jewelry, recovery, and event expansion is not implemented merely
because its designs and art assets exist.

The latest source audit found:
  - 233 of 254 definitions omit explicit inventory dimensions and default to
    1x1. Some should remain 1x1, but many larger objects are incorrectly tiny.
  - NativeBridge explicitly sets the grid shape from these dimensions, while
    NativeVisuals fits the image into that shape. A larger PNG cannot fix it.
  - The old art table itself specifies undersized bracelets, rough jade, books,
    and legacy objects; runtime and proposal dimensions also disagree.
  - Books still enter their reader through magnifier inspection.
  - The eight-book organizer currently has a 4x2 internal grid, suitable only
    for its old one-cell books.
  - Discovering a clue updates the current quote, but optional evidence selection
    can remove its effect. Deductions can consume material value rather than
    being confined to the appropriate component.
  - Discovery does not currently play the native successful-exposure sound.
  - The mod uses a separate 0..5 appraisal level, ten XP per clue, and automatic
    visible-clue discovery at 100 XP; it does not use the native career.
  - Silver-necklace structural clues were difficult to see and click at actual
    display size. Automated pixel counts did not establish visual usability.

Preserve existing asset/evidence IDs, observed facts, repairs, debts, holdings,
and customer property. Correct the behavior instead of preserving the bugs.

3. One native appraisal career

Use the base game's appraisal career, rank, promotion progress, certificates,
commission settlement, and customer flow. The locally inspected game exposes
ModHelper.AddAppraisalItem and ModHelper.GetAppraiserRank. See the audit for exact
signatures and the distinction between native appraisal and counterfeit checks.

Native rank 0 unlocks hand appraisal, rank 1 ribwich, and rank 2 plasma_fuel.
Rank 3 is master. Preserve these registrations and native progression. Register
mod families alongside them; never clear the registry or replace vanilla types.
Use unique mod-prefixed registration IDs and unregister only this mod's entries.

Implementation defaults for added commission eligibility:
  Rank 0: ordinary gold/silver goods and straightforward metal valuation.
  Rank 1: finished jade and introductory collectible appraisals.
  Rank 2: rough-jade assessments, loose-gem screening, complex collectibles,
          and registered electronics/material-recovery assessments.
  Rank 3: complex mounted-jewelry appraisals and high-value specialist commissions.
These are new-family defaults, not changes to vanilla unlock ranks. Basic trade,
reading, and ordinary repairs can be available earlier than specialist commissions.
Keep every gold form available in the product scope; do not treat this schedule
as permission to omit bracelets, necklaces, earrings, bars, or jewelry services.

Award career progress through completed, evaluated commissions, following native
accuracy and failure rules. A defect click is an observation, not a second
career reward. Do not award progress twice for native settlement plus a mod hook,
repeated evidence, reopening a UI, selling/buying back, or reloading a save.
Remove the independent click-XP mastery route and its automatic answer reveal.
Preserve native rank/progress during migration. Keep historical mod XP as legacy
data if useful; do not silently convert it into native promotions.

Use native promotion delivery for the appropriate physical handbook where
supported. If one registration needs several short volumes, implement an
idempotent entitlement/claim mechanism tied to the promotion, without duplicating
books on reload. Existing higher-rank saves receive missing entitlements once.
Opening a certificate or book is not itself permission to grant a promotion.

4. Business labels and classification

Provide visible, filterable labels: Luxury, Collectibles, Materials, and Weapons.
Retain useful Electronics and Entertainment filters for existing stock. An item
may have several labels, with one primary shelf category; labels are not mutually
exclusive substitutes for physical composition or defect families.

Examples:
  Gold jewelry: Luxury; Au-bearing setting/material; form-specific defect family.
  Rare gold jewelry: Luxury + Collectibles; collector premium requires evidence.
  Bullion: Materials + Investment; priced by measured metal, not jewelry labor.
  Finished jade/gems: Luxury; Collectibles only for supported collectible profiles.
  Rough jade/ore: Materials; saved grade/composition and uncertain public knowledge.
  Plasma fuel: Materials + Energy; preserve native type and inspection rules.
  Native hands: Weapons business grouping + Augments trait; distinguish ordinary
    prosthetic, tactical, and premium configurations without inventing new stats.
  Rare hand configurations: Weapons + Collectibles where supported by native facts.
  Circuit boards/processors: Electronics + Materials; finite recoverable metals.
  Signed books/art/rare game editions: Collectibles, with Entertainment where relevant.

Register mappings explicitly by item family/type and supported adapter. Never
infer economic behavior from translated names, substring matching, a gold-colored
sprite, or a business label alone. Gold plating is not a solid-gold mass budget.
Preserve vanilla item dimensions and native functionality when adding labels;
the new footprint table applies to StarPawn items, not a global vanilla resize.

5. Appraisal-to-sale-or-pawn customer loop

Support two compatible entry paths:
  A. A customer requests a paid appraisal. Inspect, submit a valuation, settle
     the commission once, then resolve the customer's possible follow-up intent.
  B. A customer arrives already wanting to sell or pawn. Inspect/value the item
     within that negotiation without forcing an extra paid appraisal commission.

Common flow:
  Customer arrives -> observed facts + market snapshot -> appraisal/valuation
  -> offer to sell OR request a pawn loan OR take the item away
  -> player reviews terms -> explicit acceptance -> cash/custody transfer.

The same AssetId and, where available, native unique item ID must survive this
flow. Do not spawn a copy for the follow-up offer or generate fresh defects.
Appraisal does not transfer ownership. A report is not a new collateral asset.
Commission fees, purchase payments, and pawn principal are separate ledger entries.
Let the player decline a follow-up without undoing the completed appraisal fee.

Use a saved weighted intent draw, not an unlimited repeated chance. Starting
weights, in the order leave / offer_sale / request_pawn:
  Ordinary appraisal customer: 50 / 30 / 20.
  Urgently needs cash:          15 / 35 / 50.
  Sentimentally attached:       55 /  5 / 40.
  Trade dealer:                15 / 75 / 10.
  Collector seeking advice:    70 / 15 / 15.
These are fictional balance defaults, to be exercised and tuned, not claimed
observations of the base game. Persist profile, resolved weights, snapshot, draw,
chosen intent, and offer expiry on the encounter. Reveal the outcome once after
appraisal settlement; UI reopening or save reload cannot redraw it.

Resolve pawn eligibility, customer ownership, business access, trust, and supported
item policy before drawing. Move an ineligible route's weight to leave rather
than repeatedly rerolling until a deal appears. A failed/refused appraisal need
not generate a follow-up. Finding a problem can cause a believable admission,
revised claim, refusal, or request for repair, not automatic universal agreement.

Events can shift intent weights through bounded, data-driven effects. For
example, a credit squeeze can move 15 weight points toward pawn requests, taken
from leave/sale, while a collector event changes the relevant buyer budgets and
collector demand. Sum applicable changes, clamp to nonnegative weights, normalize
once, and save the result. Each event row applies once, even across several tags.
Changes affect new encounters, not an already accepted or revealed offer.

Use finite customer budgets and actual available shop funds. Appraisal referrals
do not create extra unbounded visitors beyond the shared event/customer budget.
Keep follow-up dialogue short and contextual; no technical probability notices
are required in the ordinary customer conversation.

6. Pawn contracts as a continuation of valuation

Use the common valuation service, with a clearly displayed conservative
liquidation/buyback basis and a disclosed loan-to-value ratio. Start new offers
at 50% LTV, bounded by the planned 30..65% policy; the current 0.3 default is 60%,
so this is a new-offer balance change, not a rewrite of existing loans. Unverified
claims cannot secure a natural-gem or high-purity premium. Explain lower offers
through the missing test or liquidity limitation, not an unexplained total.

Record contract ID, customer, asset/component IDs, appraisal reference, valuation
snapshot, principal, signed interest/fees, start date, redemption deadline,
custody, and amendment history. Signing debits the shop's lendable cash once and
reserves the actual collateral. Customer collateral is unavailable for sale,
repair, disassembly, refining, another loan, or unrelated order while pledged.

Support redemption, renewal, and default/forfeiture through the same ledger.
Allow redemption/renewal through the stated deadline's business hours; evaluate
default at the documented closing boundary. Only one competing terminal action
may win. Renewal is a recorded amendment, not a duplicate live contract.
Forfeiture transfers ownership once; only then may the item enter ordinary
shop stock, repair, resale, or eligible material recovery.

Market movements affect new valuations and new agreements. Preserve principal,
rates, fees, deadlines, and agreed custody of signed contracts. No event-driven
retroactive repricing or surprise seizure. Preserve the existing player-as-
borrower route as well as the shop-as-lender route; do not confuse their cash signs.

7. Inspection, observation, and feedback

Keep direct visual inspection: click a visible defect on the item image.
Each appraisable category owns four or five fixed, illustrated defect templates
(retain the existing five-template design sets where applicable). Generate intact
items or allowed combinations from that category. Do not apply a universal pool
to unrelated forms, redraw defects randomly, or place damage outside the object.
Utility tools, cards, empty settings, and manuals have functional states, not
five contrived appraisal defects.

Store hidden item truth separately from the customer's claim and established
knowledge. Visual clues can justify suspicion; only a supported test establishes
precise content/origin. Instruments should have distinct, understandable roles:
weight, material screening/assay, magnification, jade-light observation, UV, and
gem screening. Use the limitations in the existing gem design; a handheld probe
does not prove all natural origins or treatments.

On the first correct discovery: persist the observation, immediately update all
relevant estimates, briefly mark the location, show a concise component-specific
price change, and play the native successful-exposure sound once. The audited
audio field is EmporiumAudioPlayer.audioExposeScam. Reuse its feedback without
accidentally invoking an additional native discount/settlement side effect.
No sound/reward/extra deduction for duplicate clicks, redraw, page change, or load.

Two consecutive incorrect claims against a customer end further claims for that
encounter. A new valid finding resets that streak; repeating an old finding does
not. Present complaint/withdrawal through dialogue and appropriate reputation
feedback. Persist the streak across views, instruments, book reading, and reload.
Study of player-owned stock must not impersonate a customer withdrawing an item.

All confirmed unresolved causes affect valuation automatically. Remove the
optional 'cite all evidence' workflow and evidence toggles that can restore value.
Multiple views of the same crack share one economic cause and one deduction.
Repair resolves only supported causes; history, material truth, and unrelated
damage remain. Use the authored repaired artwork and update affected reports.

8. Component valuation and connected markets

Use one valuation service for inspection, purchase, resale, appraisal assessment,
pawn collateral, workshop forecasts, and financial/material screens. Contexts
may use different disclosed bid/ask prices, margins, and liquidation haircuts;
they must agree about the item facts and the day's underlying market snapshot.

For plain precious-metal jewelry:
  metal value = measured eligible metal mass x confirmed purity x context price;
  remaining workmanship = max(0, workmanship basis - applicable craft deductions);
  item estimate = metal value + remaining workmanship.
For mounted jewelry, add the separately valued real stone components. For a
supported collectible, show any justified provenance/edition premium separately.
Do not count the gross gemstone-inclusive weight as gold weight. Unknown purity,
identity, or treatment remains explicitly estimated or untested in public UI.

Ordinary scratches, dents, repair seams, and a crack without material loss deduct
workmanship only. Cap their combined deduction at the eligible craft amount.
Under-purity/imitation changes the relevant material basis after a justified
finding. A missing metal link reduces actual metal mass even when purity is
unchanged. A missing stone removes that stone's value, not the gold mass. A loose
prong affects mounting integrity/workmanship rather than destroying the stone.
Each cause has an explicit component allocation; do not charge it again through
a generic quality multiplier over the whole total.

Example using fictional game credits: gold 600 + workmanship 200 = 800. A scratch
deducts 40 workmanship, leaving gold 600 + workmanship 160 = 760. Further cosmetic
damage may exhaust the 160, but cannot reduce the 600 gold portion. A separate
measured mass or purity change is a different operation, with its own evidence.

Keep a visible weight, confirmed/claimed fineness, today's metal buying and
selling rates, metal subtotal, craft subtotal, stone subtotal when applicable,
and final quote. Name directions from the shop's viewpoint: Buy from customer,
Sell to customer, or Material buyback. Avoid ambiguous standalone 'buy/sell'.
Clean confirmed precious metal must not fall below its eligible current buyback
value through cosmetic deductions or a blanket resale multiplier. Scrap recovery
has its own disclosed service costs; do not hide those inside clean-gold pricing.
Do not leak hidden purity through a supposedly public recovery-floor number.

Separate the routine shop reference estimate from a paid appraisal submission.
Routine trade estimates update immediately from established findings. A commission
uses the same underlying rules but lets the player submit a final estimate from
the available observations and handbook. The private correct-answer callback is
for native settlement, not an always-visible answer label. Snapshot the applicable
market for the commission so a day change cannot arbitrarily invalidate a submitted
answer. Appraisal accuracy and the later negotiated buy/pawn margin are distinct.

Use one persisted daily market and effect pipeline. Tags select registered effect
rows; apply the union of rows once per eligible component/channel. Examples:
  - Gold shock changes Au value in bullion and gold-bearing jewelry/settings.
  - Luxury demand changes relevant workmanship/willingness to pay, not Au mass.
  - Collector demand changes verified collectible premiums and buyer preferences.
  - Jade events affect the specified dou/nuo/bing bands and new rough availability.
  - Gem events change supported stone bands; mounted wrappers do not repeat them.
  - Energy disruption can affect plasma-fuel demand and new processing costs/time.
  - Weapons/augment demand targets supported hand configurations and buyers.
  - Gold prices change recoverable Au value in supported electronics, not their
    saved metal content; chip scarcity can separately change functional resale.
  - Credit conditions affect new pawn/loan terms and eligible follow-up intent.

Native goods such as fuel and hands need deliberate adapters. Adding a label or
news story alone does not change native prices. If an adapter affects an appraisal
type, its native correct-value callback, displayed observations, trade quote, and
pawn reference must use compatible rules. Preserve native functionality and scope
all hooks. No double valuation through both a native modifier and a mod modifier.

Save schedules, durations, cooldowns, forecasts, chosen options, effect IDs, daily
snapshots, and receipts. Reopening news or sleeping again after reload cannot
reroll the same day. Use the existing shared limit of three concurrent world
events and at most two extra event visitors per day as initial balance defaults.
Old opt-outs remain meaningful; installing or reading a screen is not enrollment.
Apply changed terms to new offers. Accepted loans, pawn contracts, orders, and
processing jobs retain their signed values, inputs, fees, and promises.

Study the recorded ideas from Station Events, Deep Space Financial Exchange,
Shutter Events, Deep Foundry, Integrated Chemistry Lab, and CorpPresence in
MOD_RESEARCH_04.txt. Implement appropriate original mechanics and verify any
additional claims against author sources. These references do not establish
installed-mod compatibility or permission to copy assets/code. StarPawn must work
without those mods. Optional adapters must deduplicate the same external event.

9. Product range, jewelry operations, and physical recovery

Gold: rings, bracelets, necklaces, earrings, and bars; one or two distinct designs
per form, retaining the existing two-design coverage. Retain corresponding silver
goods and silver vessels. Rough jade keeps all six existing skin families, meeting
the requested minimum of four or five. Finished jade covers pendant, cabochon,
bangle, bead strand, and small carving. Use exactly dou, nuo, and bing as the three
simplified game tiers. Skin family, grade, shape, claim, and defect are separate.

Opening/cutting does not reroll a rough stone. Children inherit saved quality and
source identity. Geometry matters: fragments cannot become a whole bangle. Keep
uncertainty until a supported observation reveals it; no secret grade encoded in
the exterior's default color or item name.

The complete jewelry business remains required: diamond, ruby, sapphire, emerald,
and pearl families; loose stones and mounted ring/pendant/earrings/bracelet/necklace;
component sourcing, screening, laboratory identification where needed, buying,
display, retail/wholesale selling, repair, demount/remount, compatible setting
fabrication, matched sets, custom orders, delivery, and recorded aftercare.

Preserve real setting and stone IDs through services. Earrings may contain two
distinct stones; a bracelet may contain several. An empty setting is not a false
missing-stone finding. Do not sell embedded children separately, recreate missing
stones by paying a repair fee, or count the wrapper's value on top of its children.
Disclosed laboratory-grown or cultured goods are not automatically counterfeit.

Customer-supplied goods stay customer-owned in service custody. Deposits are held
customer funds until earned, excluded from lendable cash and shop profit. Orders
have feasible capacity/deadlines, finite customer budgets, reserved inputs,
signed fees, refund/cancellation rules, and one delivery receipt. Follow the
existing JEWELRY_BUSINESS_04 contract where it does not conflict with Revision 05.

Physical recovery includes supported electronic components with finite, authored
Au/Ag/Cu budgets, sorting/dismantling, recovery, refining, and alloy production.
The player can compare repair/resale against recovery. All accepted operations
reserve/consume inputs and conserve each element, accounting for residue/losses.
Composition values are fictional game balance data, not real extraction yields.
Do not include real chemical extraction instructions or elemental conversion.

Machines, energy, service fees, time, ready-output capacity, and market events
must affect the actual jobs, not only the UI. Persist the accepted input/output
plan and costs. Reload and repeated collection cannot duplicate outputs. A full
inventory leaves an output safely ready to claim, not charged-and-disappeared.

12. Copy and UI throughout the business

Rewrite the whole player-facing journey, not only the manual headings. Cover item
names/tooltips, observation feedback, instrument readings, shop quotes, commission
submission, follow-up dialogue, pawn terms, redemption, workshop orders, banking,
market news, blocked actions, and receipts. Keep short, in-world language and
consistent terms, units, dates, currency, and ownership wording.

English source-copy examples (localize naturally, not word for word):
  Instead of 'Cite all evidence': no button; findings are automatically applied.
  Instead of '3 cited causes': list the actual established findings.
  Instead of 'Evidence difference': 'Workmanship reduction' or 'Metal revaluation'.
  Instead of 'Asset archive': 'Item record'.
  Instead of presenting untested purity as fact: 'Mark: Au999 | Assay: not tested'.
  Discovery: 'Surface scratch found. Workmanship -40. Current estimate: 760 Cr.'
  Clasp entry: 'Check where the clasp joins the band. An intact join is continuous;
    a break leaves a visible gap. This reduces workmanship. Metal value is
    unchanged unless material is missing.'
  Plating entry: 'A worn patch exposes a differently colored base. This suggests
    the surface and interior differ. Test the material before valuing its gold.'
  Sale follow-up: 'That is more than I expected. Would you buy it?'
  Pawn follow-up: 'I need cash, but I want this back. Can I leave it as security?'
  Leave: 'Thanks. I will keep it for now.'
  Unverified collateral: 'A material test is needed before I can lend against
    the claimed gold content.'

Differentiate a truthful customer surprised by damage, a collector, a dealer, a
cash-strapped owner, and a dishonest claimant. Do not make every correct click
produce the same cooperative response. Avoid backend terms such as schema,
state version, fixed template, atomic transaction, and snapshot in normal play.
Keep technical details in developer diagnostics. Show concrete reasons and a
useful next action when a service or transaction is unavailable.

Prefer contextual native item actions and a continuous counter conversation over
repeated F7 menu trips. Support useful filters by business label, form, ownership,
and service status. Relevant handbook and inspected item should remain usable
together. Keyboard, close/back, pagination, quote updates, and tool feedback must
agree with visible controls. No decorative buttons with no action.

13. Finance and balance constraints

Banking/finance remains included in the main mod; no master EnableFinance switch
or required third-party finance dependency. Participation is explicit: a player
who has not opened an account, signed a loan, or accepted a product gets no silent
account, credit, debt, interest, or fee. Existing obligations continue settling
until properly closed; leaving a screen or disabling an optional event is not a
debt reset.

Retain physical savings/debit and credit cards, daily transaction/credit limits,
available balance and remaining-limit displays, loans, savings interest, securities,
and existing game-only financial assets. Use meaningful, visible interest and
fees, with borrowing rates above deposit rates and no same-day deposit/borrow
loop yielding guaranteed profit. Tune the inherited 0.3 and 0.4 starting values
with economy scenarios and document the final values; do not silently overwrite
the rates on already signed contracts.

For reference, current mod/Content/finance-rules.json starts at daily savings
0.20%, credit 0.60%, loan 0.45%, and pawn 0.60%; daily deposit/withdrawal limits

14. Migration, installation, and coexistence

Before local deployment, preserve the current mod and relevant save files. Test
on an independent save or a clearly named clone. Never clear a user's entire
Mods directory, Workshop content, subscriptions, save index, or unrelated saves.
Package/install changes only in StarPawn's own location with the game closed.
Do not redistribute game binaries, personal saves, third-party mod files, or
native art/audio/font assets. Runtime references to native resources are fine.

Changing dimensions requires a real saved-inventory migration, not just new
definitions. Preserve IDs, ownership, observed evidence, repairs, balances,
contracts, stored children, and legacy handbook entitlements. Repack each affected
inventory/container with the new shapes, respecting custody. If space is short,
use a visible persistent overflow/claim location linked to the same objects.
No overlapping shapes, erased items, free copies, forced sale, or inaccessible
books. Make migration versioned and safe to resume once after interruption.

Freeze already accepted jobs/contracts to their signed versions; recompute only
new estimates after appropriate state changes. Ensure stale UI actions are
rejected before touching cash or items. Backups and a migration receipt should
make the changes inspectable. Do not overwrite migrated data with old defaults.
