StarPawn 0.4 Category-Specific Defect and Observation Contract
Design status: specification for the next art batch; NOT implemented runtime behavior.
All identifiers and English labels in DEFECT_CATALOG_04.csv are proposed authoring contracts. The playable 0.3 catalog and saved items retain their existing semantics until a separately tested migration/integration.

1. Scope and category ownership
DEFECT_CATALOG_04.csv contains 200 authored templates in 40 category/subtype sets, with exactly five templates per set.
Gold: ring, bracelet, necklace, earrings, bar. Silver: the same five, plus the existing silver cup.
Jade rough: six separate libraries for yellow, black, water, white, rust, and mountain skins. These share category jade_rough but have explicit subtype keys. A yellow-skin template must not silently appear on a black-skin model.
Finished jade: pendant, cabochon, bangle, beads, carving.
Collectibles: antique vase, painting, console, handheld, cartridge, disc, book.
Electronic scrap: board, processor, connectors.
Recovered output: refined gold bar, refined silver bar, refined copper ingot.
Ore: gold, silver, copper. Alloys: gold bar and silver bar.
This is not a universal five-defect pool reused across every item. Each category/subtype owns its IDs, object-specific anchors, visual examples, economic interpretation, and compatible profiles. Similar physical phenomena may occur in different categories, but their authored templates are independent. Two necklace models may share their category's meaning while requiring separate coordinate records and overlays for their different geometry.
Utility items, instruments, bank cards, manuals, organizers, machines, catalysts, and sealed mixed-scrap lots use their functional/status states in ITEM_LAYOUT_04.csv. Do not manufacture five appraisal defects for them. Refined and transmuted gold bars share the refined_gold_bar physical-product category; the source process remains immutable provenance, not a second visual defect pool.
The root inventory manifest is art/ITEM_LAYOUT_04.csv. Its category/subtype fields determine which template set applies. Historical compatibility art uses only the original saved condition profiles; the new category library applies to new sp4 definitions.

2. Condition selection and persistent truth
An item can be intact. A generated defective item receives one to three compatible category templates, not all five automatically. The base quality tier, composition, mass, provenance, treatment state, and selected defects are fixed when that physical item is created.
Use an explicit authored profile allow-list. It must contain intact and all five single-template states. Include at least two meaningful two-template examples and one three-template example where the geometry and economics permit them. If a category has no valid triple, document that exception rather than constructing an impossible item. Do not automatically accept all 32 bit combinations.
Each profile lists its template IDs, evidence IDs, economic-cause IDs, layer order, geometry version, and immutable material recipe reference. A creation seed chooses an allowed profile once. Opening the UI, using an instrument, achieving mastery, saving/reloading, changing market events, cutting a window, or receiving a quote never rerolls it.
The CSV's incompatible_templates field is an authoring review status, not approval of every combination. The final combinations.json must explicitly pass semantic and geometry validation for every offered combination.
Reject combinations where one object part is both absent and visibly intact, where two substitute materials occupy the same volume, where an overlay erases another required clue, or where the same fracture is simultaneously an unresolved break and a fully filled repair in that same location. A documented old repair elsewhere and a new fracture can coexist.
Templates use category-local compatibility_group values. The runtime economic CauseId must be namespaced and item-specific in evaluation. Multiple observations of one underlying problem count as one cause. For example, an exterior crack and its continuation in the window are two evidence records for one fracture, not two price deductions.

3. Three jade tiers and independent quality axes
Use exactly three simplified gameplay tiers: bean / dou (coarse), glutinous / nuo (medium), and ice / bing (fine). They are design tiers for this game rather than a complete gemological grading system. The tier identifiers are independent of the five finished shapes and six rough skins.
Tier, color family, transparency treatment, physical defects, and fraud/treatment status are separate facts. A coarse but honest intact item is possible; a fine-tier item can be cracked or treated. Do not mark all bean-tier jade as damaged or all ice-tier jade as genuine.
The initial rough skin does not reveal its exact tier or recoverable yield. Opened-window and transmitted-light artwork may show the already saved interior facts only after the relevant action. Full-grade/yield disclosure follows the gameplay observation policy in SYSTEMS_DESIGN_04.txt.
Cotton, inclusions, color concentration, and transparency are authored game-quality cues. Not every natural inclusion or attractive mottling is automatically a defect. The template is the specific documented pattern in the handbook, not every similarly colored pixel.

4. Evidence, instruments, and disclosure
The current 0.3 engine supports ViewId=front and ViewId=window. Those are the only runtime view IDs written by this CSV. The window view requires an opened jade window. It must not be selectable or discoverable before that action.
Instrument kinds reuse the current vocabulary: scale, assay, loupe, jade_light, uv, or none. Their presence in the design is not proof that 0.3 supports the proposed material or behavior. In particular, ore, copper, mixed scrap, alloy recipes, batch comparisons, and multi-material assay require new gameplay integration.
Art may supply additional loupe/UV/transmitted-light layers as proposed instrument assets. Store these separately with a declared reveal gate; do not introduce a new runtime ViewId or composite the result into the ordinary front image without corresponding code.
The visual_anchor field gives a semantic location, not a numeric hotspot. The artist must expose the relevant feature in the approved pose. For example, necklace clasps and earring posts must be visible; an authoring tool must not place their hotspots in empty space or behind an unseen side of the item.
Some defects are intentionally instrument-only: a purity deficit or short weight is not guaranteed to have a distinctive appearance. For mask_policy=unchanged, retain the intact image and supply a reading/handbook illustration separately. Never draw an arbitrary color patch to disclose hidden assay results. The actual measurement values remain runtime text, not baked artwork.
Surface cues can justify suspicion without proving exact composition. Assay and scale compare recorded truth against the claim; they do not change the truth. UV and loupe views provide authored evidence and must not claim that every fluorescence response proves fraud or that no response guarantees authenticity.
Repeated instrument use does not grant duplicate XP, reset misses, replenish customer patience, or revive a locked encounter. Instruments cannot identify an unseen window clue through the exterior view.

5. Economic interpretation and material conservation
economic_channel determines where a confirmed cause participates; it is not itself a price formula. Exact rates and event channels belong to SYSTEMS_DESIGN_04.txt.
craft_damage and structural_damage affect craftsmanship, integrity, or function. They do not erase contained precious-metal mass. Mechanical repair consumes appropriate inputs and costs, preserves the asset identity and prior observations, and resolves only its stated cause.
material_disclosure, composition_disclosure, and mass_disclosure replace unverified claims with known saved composition/mass. Never apply a second generic counterfeit deduction to the same missing metal that is already absent from the material valuation.
processability_damage affects eligible process routes, cost, recovery efficiency, or usable yield through bounded authored rules. Any actual pre-existing material loss is encoded in the creation recipe. Discovering a missing processor pin does not remove it a second time.
service_history remains a historical fact after a current defect is repaired. Polishing or replacing a port does not erase an earlier solder join, restoration, or opened-case history. An old repair is not automatically proof of fraud.
identity_disclosure, edition_disclosure, and authenticity_disclosure affect their specific claim or provenance premium; they do not arbitrarily rewrite chemical composition.
jade_quality and jade_structure feed jade quality/integrity appraisal and bounded usable-cut rules. A visible and a window view of the same fracture cannot be counted twice. Cutting partitions saved usable material and records any lost/rejected material; a new event cannot create more stone.
All CSV rows specify composition_effect=none_at_discovery. True composition changes only in an explicit processing transaction that consumes inputs and creates traceable outputs, never as a side effect of identifying a defect. Fictional alchemy follows the separate declared recipe/provenance ledger in SYSTEMS_DESIGN_04.txt.

6. Pixel art, masks, and hotspots
Keep the accepted ring's visual language: limited palette, upper-left lighting, deliberate pixel clusters, restrained highlights, and a separately authored small inventory image. Inventory footprints and exact pixel dimensions come exclusively from art/ITEM_LAYOUT_04.csv. The defect catalog never overrides a canvas or footprint.
same_alpha means the intact and defective state have identical alpha, with the defect layer wholly inside the opaque object region. This is suitable for scratches, plating patches, surface fissures, and many treatment cues.
state_mask permits a physically justified change to the silhouette, such as a chip, missing pin, open chain link, or bent post. Keep the same canvas, origin, camera, and object registration. Deliver a separate approved state mask and alpha-changing replacement layer. No detached floating damage marks or pixels outside the authored state object are allowed.
unchanged means no ordinary-view artwork difference is warranted. Use the intact sprite and instrument/record feedback; do not fabricate visible clues.
A closed fracture line that does not remove material may preserve alpha; a missing chip must not be painted as a dark sticker while falsely claiming identical geometry. State-mask changes must also update the relevant geometry check expectations and all condition composites.
For every model, state, and visible view, hotspots.json must provide center coordinates U/V and full Width/Height normalized to the actual artwork canvas. Instrument-only observations instead record their reveal gate and a null visual region; do not invent a clickable target on an intact sprite. The origin is top-left. Do not confuse a top-left origin with rectangle-top-left fields.
Correct conversion from pixel bounds [x0,x1),[y0,y1): U=(x0+x1)/(2*canvas_width), V=(y0+y1)/(2*canvas_height), Width=(x1-x0)/canvas_width, Height=(y1-y0)/canvas_height.
The existing Claude ring generator's bbox_norm() currently exports x0/width and y0/height as U/V. Fix that export and regenerate the proposal before adoption. Recompute from integer bounds; do not copy rounded hand-adjusted values.
Keep hit rectangles tight enough to match authored evidence. A rectangle must not accidentally include a distant neighboring defect or turn empty canvas into a successful hit. If a concave/missing-part defect needs a mask-aware hit rule, declare that as required integration rather than silently widening the rectangle.

7. Legacy migration and explicit mappings
legacy_reference names the existing 0.3 template/evidence for a reviewer to compare. It is not an alias instruction and does not authorize renaming an existing saved evidence ID. A metal_wear reference may map to different new category-specific meanings; these require new sp4 profiles.
Existing sp3 and legacy DefinitionId values retain their original defect sets, material truth, saved observations, and XP history. Do not add new causes to an existing saved definition merely because new artwork exists. Geometry-only updates can preserve IDs if texture, hotspot, and handbook example change together and all existing conditions remain equivalent.
New categories, new defect meanings, or expanded combinations receive new definition/profile IDs and an explicit versioned mapping. The game may choose new sp4 profiles for newly created stock after integration; it must not restock previously purchased old stock during the same day.
Do not rewrite old certificates, native item IDs, family ownership, embedded materials, or consumption history during art import. A certificate describes the observations and date at issuance; later market events change current quotes, not historical records.

8. Required art delivery and acceptance
For each new model deliver an intact inspection image, intact inventory image, five separately authored defect examples, layered/editable sources, masks, per-defect overlays, compatible profile manifest, and hotspots. Include repaired appearances where supported, preserving nonrepairable history/treatment states.
Supply front/window or gated instrument examples only where relevant. All art files must use the fixed dimensions in ITEM_LAYOUT_04.csv. Create handbook example crops from the same final evidence layers and positions; do not paint unrelated generic textbook marks.
Each category must have its own five handbook explanations with the affected part, observation/tool gate, likely price consequence, repairability, and what the clue does not establish. English documentation accompanies Chinese runtime localization keys; no long explanatory text is baked into UI skins.
Art checks: no wrong dimensions; correct shared registration; masks match policy; no floating defect pixels; overlays reproduce approved composites; numeric hotspots use centers; all visible clue pixels fall within the intended region; no hidden-interior leakage; no duplicated or missing template IDs.
Gameplay integration checks: each profile is deterministic after reload; same-cause observations deduct once; invalid combinations are rejected; old saves keep old truth; instrumentation is gated by ownership/view/encounter lock; repair does not remove plating or historical evidence; processed outputs conserve recipe/provenance; market events change valuations/conditions of service rather than saved item truth.
This document is a batch-art and future-integration specification. Producing the PNGs does not by itself implement new defect logic, new measurements, new material recipes, or the 0.4 migration.
