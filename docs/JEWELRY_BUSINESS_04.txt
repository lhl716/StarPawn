StarPawn 0.4 Jewelry Business Contract
Revision 04.2 / 2026-11-13 / PLANNED, NOT IMPLEMENTED

1. Scope and authority
The requested jewelry business means product categories and a complete shop loop. It is not a request to install a mod named Jewelry Business. Gold and jade alone did not cover this loop in revision 04.1; this document supplies the missing gemstone and mounted-jewelry design.
Read with SYSTEMS_DESIGN_04.txt, JEWELRY_SETTINGS_04.csv, JEWELRY_OPERATIONS_04.csv and the frozen art manifests. All values below are fictional starting balance values. No real market feed, investment advice or chemical procedure is intended.
No fictional alchemy or elemental transmutation is in scope. Metal recovery only separates existing material; assembly combines actual settings and actual stones. Gem synthesis, magical gem upgrades, raw-gem cutting and a new mining simulator are outside this bounded 0.4 release. Existing jade cutting retains its separate three-tier contract.

2. Products and fixed inventory footprints
Loose stones: diamond (round/princess), ruby (oval/cushion), sapphire (oval/pear), emerald (step/oval), pearl (round/baroque). Ten authored models, all 1x1 / 64x64 inventory; each has a 384x256 inspection image. A pearl is treated as a pearl product, not a faceted crystal.
Mounted products: solitaire ring 1x1, bezel pendant 1x1, paired stud earrings 1x1, trilogy bracelet 2x1, drop necklace 2x2. Each has one authored setting design, two metal appearances (18K gold and 925 silver) and the exact compatible stone variants in JEWELRY_SETTINGS_04.csv. Metal appearances do not change cell count. Existing plain gold/silver forms remain separate products with their own defects and two designs each.
Five empty-setting components retain the exact corresponding mounted footprint. An empty setting is a service component, not a complete piece with a falsely reported missing stone. Settings preserve their metal composition and repair history through demounting and reassembly.
Support: gem screening probe 2x1; jeweler workbench 2x2; toolkit 2x1; gem case 2x2 with 4x4 internal grid; presentation case 2x2 with 4x2 internal grid; display tray 2x2 with 4x4 internal grid; identification report and job ticket each 1x1. No nested containers. All child assets keep their IDs and custody.
There are nine category manuals plus the legacy guide. Retain the original eight-slot organizer unchanged. Add a distinct twelve-slot organizer: 2x2 exterior, 4x3 interior. These are different item definitions, not a silent capacity upgrade to an existing container.

3. Saved truth, knowledge and custody
GemRecord: StoneAssetId, FamilyId, ShapeId, MassUg, DiameterMicrons (pearls), FitClass, OriginId, TreatmentIds[], SavedQualityProfileId, DefectCauseIds[], Claim, ObservedFacts[], LabReportIds[], ProvenanceId, Version.
OriginId distinguishes natural, laboratory_grown, cultured (pearls) and imitation. These are identity facts, not automatic defects. A disclosed laboratory-grown stone or cultured pearl can be sold honestly. Imitation templates describe a particular mismatch against a claim, not every inexpensive stone. Use registered identity price bands; do not pretend a handheld probe proves natural origin or every treatment.
Use weight in carats for faceted gems and diameter plus recorded mass for pearls; 1 ct = 0.2 g. Three simple presentation quality profiles (commercial/select/fine) summarize saved appearance; they are GAME profiles, not laboratory grades or the jade tier IDs. Color, clarity/cut cues and treatment remain separate fields. No arbitrary new grading tree is required for art.
SettingRecord: SettingAssetId, FormId, MetalComposition, FitRequirements[], MechanicalCauseIds[], ServiceHistory[], Version.
JewelryAssembly: AssemblyAssetId, SettingAssetId, SocketBindings[{SocketId, StoneAssetId}], MissingSocketIds[], OwnershipId, CustodyId, Claim, Reports[], Version.
A pair of earrings is one merchandise assembly with two sockets and two distinct stone IDs. A trilogy bracelet has three sockets and three stones. A printed gem, repeated sprite or socket never creates material. The aggregate valuation reads children; it does not add an assembly-level duplicate of their masses.
When mounted, child stones/settings exist as embedded, untradeable records. Demounting retires the assembly wrapper and makes the SAME component IDs loose again, retaining observations and damage. Reassembly creates a new wrapper with provenance links. Repair of an existing wrapper changes its version and records actual supplied metal/loss; it preserves identity. Only one active custody location is permitted per physical asset.
Keep metal composition in the setting and stone mass in GemRecord. Total assembly mass = setting mass + sum(stone masses) + registered nonmetal support mass. Do not also count stone mass as setting inert material. Metal processing rejects occupied settings; first demount the stones. Pearls and stones cannot be furnace feedstock or sold twice as both material and embedded goods.

4. The shop loop
A. Source: customers, finite suppliers, event visitors and disclosed used lots offer actual saved objects. Save stock, quantities, claims and defects before showing the offer. Refreshing a page or reopening a case cannot reroll or restock it. The same goods can be sold intact, serviced or broken down; these alternatives consume/reserve the same ownership record.
B. Inspect: read the category handbook; examine the setting and each stone; weigh, screen and if necessary commission a laboratory report. Loupe/UV/probe give their authored limited observations. Unknown origin/treatment remains unknown. Laboratory testing is a paid one-day service with item custody, a locked quote and a version-bound report. Reports are not new physical gemstones or tradeable gold.
The gem probe has only supported screening outcomes: refer_for_lab, no_obvious_substitute_detected, unsupported or no_reading. Passing a screen does not confirm natural origin, an exact quality tier or an absence of treatment. Published per-family observation rules must state which claim can actually be challenged.
C. Quote and buy: show known identity, gross/metal/gem weights, claim uncertainty, Au/Ag bid/ask, stone value and workmanship separately. Record SnapshotId, all child versions and a finite offer expiration. Confirm one atomic cash/item transfer. Purchase estimates never reveal saved facts the player has not learned.
D. Service: choose repair, demount, fabricate an empty setting or mount/re-mount known compatible stones. Compare the cost, time, spare material, expected resale and remaining uncertainty before acceptance. Equipment and funds must exist. Missing sockets require real replacement stones; no fee alone recreates one.
E. Stock and display: the tray holds actual inventory children with disclosed sale descriptions, reserves items under a live offer, and releases an expired reservation once. Moving goods does not copy stock or reissue reports. Sale prices cannot advertise an unconfirmed origin as certain.
F. Sell: wholesale buyback uses the current conservative bid; retail customers have finite budgets and preferences. A sale atomically transfers every child and any explicitly included case/report. There is no infinitely funded premium buyer.
G. Match a set: associate compatible ring/pendant/earrings or another listed request with a set record containing the real AssetIds. Set demand can add at most 5% of the combined workmanship, only for a customer who requested a match; it never multiplies contained metal or stones. Unpack preserves children and cannot retain the original sellable set. If physical space is insufficient, keep goods separate and quote the set by IDs.
H. Custom order: request -> inspect customer property -> price and material selection -> accepted escrow -> scheduled work -> ready -> confirmed delivery -> closed receipt. Orders can use customer-owned stones/settings or disclosed shop-owned ingredients. Customer property remains customer-owned in custody, unavailable for sale, pawn, recovery or unrelated jobs. Receipt reprints and save reloads cannot restart an order or redeem it twice.
I. Aftercare: report-linked repair/return requests identify the original sale and current item versions. Cosmetic polishing does not erase treatment, origin or structural history. A replacement creates a recorded component substitution with the old component returned or separately consumed. Do not add an unrestricted refund-on-demand exploit.

5. Pricing and event integration
DailyEconomySnapshot gains GemBidAsk[FamilyId, OriginId, QualityProfileId] and a registered gem_price channel (combined factor 6000..16000). Prices are per carat for faceted stones; pearl quotes use a saved diameter/quality band per pearl. Stone shapes and known condition modify their own component quote once. A no-origin claim receives a published conservative range until identified, not the natural-stone premium.
Starting unadjusted asks in Cr per ct for commercial natural stones: diamond 120, ruby 90, sapphire 70, emerald 85. Select/fine factors: 15000/22000 bps. Initial wholesale bid is 8000 bps of the adjusted ask. Laboratory-grown bands use 4000 bps of the corresponding natural base where a family supports them; imitation is a separately registered low-value material profile, never the natural baseline. Cultured pearls use commercial/select/fine asks of 8/14/24 Cr per pearl at the reference diameter band. Other pearl origins require explicit profiles before offers can spawn them. These numbers are starting gameplay data, not real valuations.
Example known 1 ct commercial natural diamond: ask 120 Cr, bid 96 Cr at neutral market. A +10% gem event changes the pair to ask 132 / bid 105.6 before transaction rounding. It does not change carats, origin or flaws. A customer workmanship event affects only workmanship.
Mounted retail reference = fine-metal ask value + sum(known stone ask values after their own causes) + condition-adjusted workmanship * category_demand. Wholesale reference = fine-metal bid value + sum(eligible known stone bid values) + conservative remaining workmanship. Apply each cause to one eligible component once. A missing stone contributes zero stone value; it does not also reduce the metal budget. A loose prong reduces integrity/workmanship, not the saved diamond mass.
Starting intact workmanship bases in Cr: ring 12, pendant 10, paired earrings 16, bracelet 24, necklace 20. Retail willingness caps the premium; customers do not owe every optimistic quote. Gold-floor rules still apply to eligible actual fine gold at the shared buyback bid. For a separate scrap route, show required demount/recovery charges and preserve loose stones; never hide an arbitrary fee in clean-metal buyback.
gem_freight: temporary ruby/sapphire/emerald supply and price changes. jewelry_festival: diamond/pearl component demand, separate workmanship demand and finite custom orders. gem_disclosure: paid lab service discount and preference for valid reports. All use the ordinary saved event queue, cooldowns and visitor cap. Gold/silver shocks affect settings; gem shocks affect stones; power/freight shocks affect new workshop quotes. Existing signed orders and jobs keep their agreed fees, input allocations, output plans and deadlines.
Every quote records the snapshot and all bound component versions. Combining category tags takes the union of effect rows, then clamps once per channel. Do not apply the same gemstone event again to the assembled item, set packaging, or gold/silver portion. All quote/buyback/repair/order screens explain changes from the same snapshot.

6. Workshop operations and conservation
JEWELRY_OPERATIONS_04.csv freezes initial routes, base fees, durations, energy and required state. Jewelry jobs participate in the same job, energy and receipt system as recovery. Prices use the service/energy factors of SYSTEMS_DESIGN_04.txt. Lab service is externally scheduled and reserves custody, not a physical workshop slot.
Fabricate setting: select 18K Au/Cu or 925 Ag/Cu and supply enough measured alloy for the listed target setting mass. Allocate the exact weighted components; return excess in measured lots. Initial locked efficiency is 10000 bps with all shaping scrap returned, so repeated manufacture is never a metal gain. It still costs service, energy and time. A compatible empty setting can also be bought from a finite supplier.
Demount: release the same stones and setting, with all history retained. A disclosed difficult/damaged mount may require a repair first. For this release do not introduce an undisclosed random stone-destruction roll; reject unsupported states with a specific reason. A missing stone is already absent, so no stone can be recovered from that socket.
Mount: verify family, shape, size class, socket count, ownership, condition and declared finish. Use one supplied stone per required socket. A compatible incomplete restoration requires an explicitly shown missing-socket state; it cannot be mislabeled complete. New-stock complete assemblies must fill every socket.
Repair: resolves only supported mechanical causes, with exact additive alloy supplied if the repair needs material. Preserve treatment and gem causes. Chip/inclusion polishing or recutting is outside this release; do not generate a repaired flawless stone state. Stones are protected in job custody, not deleted by the metal repair.
All operations preflight output grid capacity at collection. A full inventory leaves the result ready and uniquely claimable. Input escrow, fees, energy, material allocations and child ownership commit atomically. A failed preflight consumes nothing. Per-component conservation is required; there is no elemental-conversion exception.

7. Orders, settlement and initial balance limits
At most three accepted jewelry customer orders at once, one active/ready job per workbench, and the shared 12-unit nightly energy reservation limit. Maximum two extra event visitors per day remains global, not two more jewelry visitors on top of it. One supplier has at most two offers per day; the generated stock is persisted. Initial ordinary order deadline is acceptance day +3; a new quote must fit the current capacity and disclosed delay before accepting.
Service-only order (customer materials): quote a fixed service fee and disclosed optional parts; take 30% of the service fee as a deposit. Commission order (shop materials): reserve the selected owned inputs and take 30% of the complete agreed price as a deposit. No speculative order may reserve materials the shop does not own or has already pledged. Remaining balance is collected exactly once at delivery; the customer budget is committed at acceptance.
For integer money, Deposit=floor(Price*3000/10000); Balance=Price-Deposit. The deposit is a refundable customer liability until earned at delivery, not profit, interest-bearing cash or lending collateral. Display available cash separately from held customer funds. Record quote price, inputs, ownership, deadline, promised disclosure and refund schedule in the signed OrderRecord.
Player cancellation before work begins: release inputs, return customer property and refund the full deposit. After work begins: return available customer property and shop parts to their proper owners, refund deposit less ONLY the previously disclosed completed service cost capped at the deposit; remaining loss is the shop's. A station fault pauses the job and offers a free revised date or full deposit refund. No silent forfeiture, seizure of customer stones or retroactive price increase. Event changes alone cannot create a penalty for an already signed order.
Delivery closes the order and releases the held deposit into earned revenue while collecting the exact balance. Include COGS for consumed shop ingredients, service/grid fees, energy acquisition cost and case cost; a margin display must not count customer stones as shop profit. Wrong item, duplicate delivery, stale report or missing child rejects before transfer. Unclaimed ready goods remain owned/custodied as recorded; no automatic resale after a timer.
For matching-set delivery use one atomic transfer of the listed pieces, not one payment per UI child plus another set payment. Accept only a feasible deadline and finite order budget. General finance/pawn rules cannot use customer escrow as collateral, reset daily card limits or create an implicit loan.

8. Appraisal, art and acceptance
Five loose-gem families and five mounted forms each own five fixed templates in DEFECT_CATALOG_04.csv. Gem defects belong to StoneAssetId; mounting defects belong to SettingAssetId or an explicit missing socket. Do not reroll children when making an assembly. A composition may expose both, but its combined defect list and price effects retain distinct CauseIds. The 1-3 generation rule applies per newly created component, not as a permission to discard inherited evidence.
The mounted artwork uses real stone layers; a missing socket masks only that stone. Frozen poses show each prong, clasp, post and stone clue that the template needs. Art delivery includes gold/silver bases, every supported stone variant, empty settings, five mounting defects, legal inherited stone overlays, and repaired mechanical states. Layer composition prevents an unnecessary explosion of duplicate full PNG permutations. Never reveal hidden origin through a color-coded default texture.
Manuals follow art/VANILLA_HANDBOOK_SPEC_04.txt: actual vanilla-style portrait reference sheets, category-local side-by-side examples, short explanation, tool gate and interpretation limits. No live-item answer sheet, invented analysis dashboard or generic five-flaw page pasted across categories.
Required implementation tests: material/stone conservation through demount/remount; no duplicate ownership; paired earrings with two unique stones; missing socket yields nothing; all family/shape/fit gates; assay knowledge separation; reference/fault art agreement; immutable order terms; deposit/refund/delivery idempotency; full inventory and native spawn rollback; finite stock and visitor caps; snapshot pricing parity; no double-counting event/cause/set premium; old saves unchanged. Design validation alone does not prove any of these runtime paths implemented.
