StarPawn Gameplay Reference Research
Reviewed: 2026-11-13

Primary reference: Deep Space Financial Exchange (original title: "深空金融交易所 Exchange：股票·大宗"), by Rinka
Author's release page: https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3829559307
This review used only the author's public gameplay description. No binaries or source code were downloaded or merged.

Useful ideas: a physical terminal as an entry point; separate areas for daily quotes and holdings; overnight settlement summaries; price alerts; links between events and market information; and explanations of committed funds. The author's page also describes limit orders, derivative trading, and securities-backed loans. These are not all included in StarPawn's first revision.
StarPawn adaptation: assets and money continue to use StarPawn's own unified ledger, and jewelry and gold bars reference the same daily prices. Clear account limits and settlement information come first; complex securities features can then be evaluated individually.
Difference: StarPawn preserves its established save determinism and persistent defects. It must not directly adopt another project's market-reroll rules.

The financial mod is not among the mods installed on this machine. Coexistence testing may be considered later; an uninstalled project has not been presented as compatibility-tested.
Other candidates: Faction Stocks (3828925366) and Moneylending (3830603988). Their full author pages were not obtained in this review, so no implementation conclusions are based on them.

Source-use boundary: study gameplay ideas and implement them independently. Permission to copy the author's source code or art has not been confirmed; third-party files are not included in this repository.
